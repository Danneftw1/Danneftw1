# Direction B — Tryck

A proposal, not implemented. Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/2bVTHFQD4EcCCW2RUGGxD2
(boards "B · Tryck", 896 and 390 px, light and dark). Written 2026-10-05.

## The idea

The profile as printed matter: a masthead, a dateline, hairline rules, a record sleeve's track
list for the record, numbered entries for what is current, a colophon for the stack, footnotes
for the archive. It is set, not drawn: neither today's neon machine nor Stilla's bare page.

## Layout

- 896 px: padding 72 / 72 / 80. Dateline row (Göteborg · AI engineer, full-stack · No. 1 · 2026)
  over a 1 px rule; masthead "Daniel / Nilsson" at 92 px / 800, −0.035 em, opsz 144; a double
  rule (3 px over 1 px). Then two columns (48 px gutter): the intro with a drop cap, and the
  Record as a four-row track list (A1, A2, B1, B2 · when · what · detail) between rules.
  Now as a numbered two-column list (01–06). Colophon and Older as two narrow-grotesk footnotes.
- 390 px: one column; masthead 60 px; the title line moves under the masthead; the track list
  and the numbered list stack.
- Rules: 1 px ink between sections, 1 px light grey between track rows.

## Tokens

- Light: paper `#ffffff`, ink `#1c1a17`, grey `#5a5651`, hairline `#d9d5ce`, spot `#c8371e`.
- Dark: page `#0d1117`, ink `#ebe7df`, grey `#a8a39a`, hairline `#3a3f47`, spot `#ff6a4d`.
- Type: Fraunces (display and body, 400 / 600 / 800, optical size) and Archivo Narrow
  (labels, dateline, footnotes, 400 / 600), both Google Fonts.
- The spot colour appears only on numbers, section labels, the drop cap and link underlines.

## States

None interactive beyond links; nothing animates.

## What may never change

1. One spot colour, and only on numbers, labels, the drop cap and underlines. Never on body text.
2. Rules are hairlines: 1 px, and the one double rule under the masthead.
3. Nothing moves.
4. Every fact is the README's; the track-list numbering (A1 … B2) is layout, not a claim.
5. 390 px: no horizontal scroll; the two-column sections stack.

## If chosen

Implementation is `README.md` plus one static SVG for the masthead and dateline (the only part
that needs the typeface), generated by `tools/build-assets.py`; the track list, numbered list
and footnotes are Markdown tables and lists.
